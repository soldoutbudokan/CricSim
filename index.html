A few balls to find your feet

Meet the ball

Watch the bowler’s hand, read the bounce, and bring the bat to the ball. The bat is regulation width. Start at half speed if you need more time.

MousePosition the bat across the crease and up or down. The ring marks the middle of the blade. LMB + moveHold, then push the mouse to drive the bat through the ball. More travel is more reach; faster travel is more bat speed. Release to reset. RMBHold for a soft-handed block. Move the blade to meet the ball, or move it aside to leave. AD WSTurn the face and add loft. QE roll the blade for cuts and pulls. C resets all three. Space PBowl the next ball · pause or resume. Conditions apply to the next delivery.

Touch: drag to position and play. A mouse and keyboard give full control. Pace and movement use a physical approximation; this is an early simulation, not a coaching instrument.
